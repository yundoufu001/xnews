Mollick：自主 AI 研究进展与经济价值任务进展未必同步

Ethan Mollick 说，追踪 AI 能自主完成多少工作很关键，但只盯最顶尖机构的研究任务会低估 AI 在经济价值任务上的能力；MirrorCode 显示 AI 能自主复现人类估计要花 2 到 17 周的软件，OpenAI 报告里研究者估计编程智能体把一个月的工作量压到了三分之一。

Ethan Mollick（@emollick）· 原帖 2026-11-21T02:51:40+00:00 · 收录 2026-11-22T08:25:00+08:00

Ethan Mollick 在帖子里说，追踪 AI 能自主完成多少工作很关键，这类衡量能对冲关于自主 AI 研究现状最夸张的说法。但他也提醒，只盯最顶尖 AI 研究机构的研究任务，会低估 AI 在经济价值任务上已经能做到的事，两类任务应该分开看。他认为发帖的 Ramez 并没有把两者混为一谈。

研究任务与经济价值任务要分开看

大多数工作不是前沿 AI 研究，在那里 AI 能非常成功。Epoch 的 MirrorCode 结果就是一个例子：AI 自主复现软件，研究者估计人类要花 2 到 17 周。这类工作定义明确，边界条件很不一样。

有人参与也不耽误提效

现实世界里偶尔有人介入完全可以接受，在他看来，有人的角色比完全没人好得多。偶尔指导也能带来巨大的生产率提升，即使这达不到完全自主 AI 研究的要求。同一份 OpenAI 报告里，研究者估计，团队一个月的活，没有编程智能体平均要花三个月。

两类进展未必同步

总之，递归自我改进或 AI 驱动的研究进展，未必与经济价值任务的进展紧密挂钩，后一类任务的方差和范围了解得还不够。读者要用的话，落点可以放在分开衡量上：自主研究任务的成绩单，不能直接当成经济价值任务的成绩单。

英文原文（完整保留）

I think trying to track how much work AI can do autonomously is critical, and this is a useful counter to some of the most audacious claims about the state of autonomous AI research. But I do want to note that the focus on research tasks at the most advanced AI research organizations on the planet also runs the risk of substantially underestimating what AI can already do in terms of economically valuable tasks. Its worth separating the two (and I don't think Ramez is claiming they are not seperated).

Most work is not frontier AI research, and there AI can be tremendously successful. Epoch's MirrorCode results, for example, show AI autonomously reimplementing software that the researchers estimate would take a humans 2-17 weeks. This is well-specified work, so it has very different boundary conditions.

Plus, in the real world, some human intervention is fine (in my mind, vastly preferrable to no human role for a variety of reasons). Occasional guidance can still leave you with enormous productivity gains, even if that doesn’t satisfy the requirements for fully autonomous AI research. And even in the case of AI research, the same OpenAI report includes researchers estimating that a month of their team’s work would have taken three months without coding agents, on average.

In short, RSI/AI-driven research progress may not tightly couple with progress on economically valuable tasks, and the variance and range on those tasks are less well understood.
