Gemini 智能体找到更快量子算法，三类模拟算力成本降 2.5 到 10.9 倍

Pushmeet Kohli 宣布，Gemini 驱动的智能体在量子电路优化上找到新算法，核聚变模拟、光化学、药物代谢分子模拟的估算算力成本分别降低 10.9 倍、8.1 倍和 2.5 倍，每条量子电路结果都经 Lean 数学证明。

Pushmeet Kohli（@pushmeet）· 原帖 2026-11-23T17:14:12+00:00 · 收录 2026-11-24T08:28:00+08:00

Google DeepMind 的 Gemini 智能体在量子算法上拿出新结果。Pushmeet Kohli 11 月 23 日分享，智能体找到的新算法把三类模拟的估算算力成本压了下来：核聚变模拟降 10.9 倍，光化学降 8.1 倍，药物代谢相关分子模拟降 2.5 倍。目标是让这些算法更早跑上量子硬件。

每条结果都经过 Lean 数学证明

与此前 AlphaProof 软件验证突破类似，每条量子电路结果都在 Lean 里得到数学证明。证明的不是近似效果，是结果本身在数学上成立。Kohli 把这一点放在帖子核心：智能体提效的同时，正确性有形式化验证兜底。

智能体压缩的是以年计的工作量

这套智能体方法把过去需要数年的工作压缩下来，研究者可以把精力放在定义问题和解读结果上。Kohli 也划了边界：实用的量子模拟需要的硬件比第一代纠错机更强，效率提升有前景，但还没到终点。

对读者来说，后续看点在博客与技术论文

团队把细节与技术论文链接放在 DeepMind 博客里。读的时候可以直接看两个点：三类模拟的成本数字是不是估算口径，以及 Lean 证明覆盖了哪些电路结果。

Gemini-powered agents are accelerating discoveries across our @GoogleDeepMind Science missions.

Today, our team is sharing how this paradigm is able to increase the efficiency of quantum circuits, bringing forward the implementation of these algorithms on quantum hardware. 💻

Our agents found new algorithms that cut estimated compute costs by 10.9x for a fusion simulation, 8.1x for photochemistry, and 2.5x for simulating a molecule involved in drug metabolism. Similar to our recent software verification breakthrough with AlphaProof, each quantum circuit result is mathematically proved in Lean .

This agentic-powered method compresses work that once took years, and empowers researchers to focus on defining problems and interpreting results.

There is certainly more work to do — practical quantum simulations will require more powerful hardware than first-generation error-corrected machines — but these efficiency gains are promising.

Congrats to @benjaderberg, Johannes Bausch, @SergeyShir994 and the broader @GoogleDeepMind and @GoogleQuantumAI teams who collaborated on this project.

Read our blog for more details and a link to our technical paper: https://deepmind.google/blog/finding-faster-quantum-algorithms-with-agents-and-formal-verification/
