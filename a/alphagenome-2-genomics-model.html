DNA 变异在不同细胞里怎么起作用，AlphaGenome 2 一次看清数百种细胞类型

Pushmeet Kohli 发布 AlphaGenome 2：预测按细胞类型和状态调整，在 TenK10K 数据上因果变异检出率从 16% 升到 30%，方向性基因效应从 23% 升到 43%。

Pushmeet Kohli（@pushmeet）· 原帖 2026-11-11T15:25:56+00:00 · 收录 2026-11-11T23:55:00+08:00

Pushmeet Kohli 介绍了 AlphaGenome 2，Google DeepMind 的新一代基因组模型，专门解读 DNA 变异如何影响细胞。细胞是生命的基本单位，也是理解生物学的关键。预测 DNA 变异会如何改变不同细胞的功能，一直是个难题，比如心肌细胞和正在对抗感染的免疫细胞，同一个变异在它们身上可能有完全不同的后果，疾病背后的细胞驱动因素也藏在其中。

预测先看细胞类型和状态

AlphaGenome 2 的架构设计把预测和细胞类型、状态绑在了一起。模型能预测变异在数百种人类细胞类型中的效应，还能在不重新训练的情况下，适应没见过的细胞和疾病状态。条件变了，预测跟着变，这是这一代和上一代的核心差别。

TenK10K 免疫细胞数据上，因果变异检出率近乎翻倍

在 TenK10K 免疫细胞数据集上，AlphaGenome 2 的准确度比上一代有了大幅提升。它可靠识别出的因果变异从 16% 升到 30%，方向性基因效应从 23% 升到 43%。两项指标都接近翻倍，意味着研究人员能以更高的置信度，找出以前被忽略的疾病相关变异。

回顾病例：ETS2 与肠道疾病的因果变异

Kohli 还用回顾性病例说明了模型的实际用途。一项标志性研究此前发现，一个远端调控开关里的变异会过度激活肠道巨噬细胞中的 ETS2 基因，从而驱动肠易激疾病。AlphaGenome 2 在预测肠道多种免疫细胞类型的变异效应时，准确找出了那个因果变异，并显示它只在肠道巨噬细胞里选择性地增强 ETS2 基因，对其他细胞类型和基因基本没有影响。

非商业学术研究开放使用

AlphaGenome 2 现在通过 AlphaGenome API 向非商业学术研究开放，之后还会在 Google Antigravity 里发布 AlphaGenome 2 Skills。Kohli 致谢了同事 Adam Gayoso、Žiga Avsec 和整个基因组学团队。

想判断这套模型对自己的研究有没有用，先看官方博客里 TenK10K 的具体评测口径和回顾病例的完整链条，再决定要不要用 API 跑自己的变异数据。

英文原文（完整保留）

I’m very pleased to introduce AlphaGenome 2, @GoogleDeepMind’s next-generation genomics model that deciphers how DNA changes affect cells.

Cells are the basic units of life and key to our understanding of biology. It is an ongoing challenge to predict how DNA changes affect the functioning of different cells, such as for a heart muscle cell or an immune cell fighting an infection, and to uncover cellular drivers of disease.

To make progress on this challenge, we needed to design the architecture of AlphaGenome 2 so its predictions were conditional on cell types and states. This new design enables AlphaGenome 2 to predict variant effects across hundreds of human cell types and adapt to unseen cell and disease states without retraining.

When evaluated on the TenK10K immune cell dataset, AlphaGenome 2 demonstrated a major leap in accuracy over its predecessor. It reliably identified nearly twice as many causal variants (30% vs. 16%) and directional gene effects (43% vs. 23%) compared to the original AlphaGenome. This step-change improvement in precision helps researchers pinpoint previously overlooked disease-relevant variants with high confidence.

Using analysis on retrospective case studies, we were able to show AG2’s real world utility in disease understanding. A landmark study had previously shown that a variant in a distant regulatory switch drives irritable bowel disease by overactivating the ETS2 gene in gut macrophages. When used to make predictions across various immune cell types in the gut, AlphaGenome 2 singled out the exact causal variant and showed that it selectively boosts the ETS2 gene in gut macrophages, leaving other cell types and genes largely unaffected.

I would like to thank my colleagues Adam Gayoso @adamgayoso, Žiga Avsec @Avsecz, and our entire genomics team for their incredible contributions and dedication to bringing this model to life.

We are making AlphaGenome 2 available for non-commercial academic research via the AlphaGenome API, and soon we will release AlphaGenome 2 Skills in Google Antigravity. I’m incredibly excited to see how scientists around the world use this model to make progress on biological understanding and treating disease.
