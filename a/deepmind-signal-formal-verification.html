一个月验完 Signal 的生产代码，DeepMind 把形式化验证做到了设备上的机器码

Pushmeet Kohli 宣布，Google DeepMind 用新框架 AlphaProof Code 证明了 Signal 协议底层机器码的关键安全性质，并把形式化规范与验证产物全部开源。

Pushmeet Kohli（@pushmeet）· 原帖 2026-11-10T12:13:07+00:00 · 收录 2026-11-11T17:00:00+08:00

同类工作过去常被估算为几十年的人力投入，Google DeepMind 这次用时一个月。他们证明了 Signal 消息协议底层机器码的关键安全性质。Pushmeet Kohli 在帖中说，这是广泛部署的协议第一次有实际生产代码被形式化验证到这个深度，用没用 AI 的方法都算在内。

形式化验证贵在哪

普通测试只能抽查代码在若干输入下的表现，形式化验证要求用数学证明把性质钉死，标准更高，代价也更高。Kohli 的说法是，它极其复杂、极其耗时，很多场合直接被判定为不切实际。一个月对几十年，差距主要来自这次用的工具。

AlphaProof Code 验到了设备这一层

完成验证的是 AlphaProof Code，DeepMind 新推出的形式化验证智能体框架，运行在包括 Gemini Carbon 在内的大语言模型上。验证对象没有停在协议描述或参考实现，而是下沉到真正在设备上运行的代码。这一点决定了结论的含金量：协议写得对，不等于装到手机上的那份代码也对，这次验的是后者。

Kohli 把这件事的意义放在防御一侧：现代网络威胁的速度和规模在涨，前沿 AI 在这里成了放大防御能力的杠杆。他给出的后续图景是，医疗设备固件、电网软件这类关键系统，将来可以带着数学证明出厂，证明自家的防护确实成立。

规范和验证产物全部开源

DeepMind 把形式化规范和验证产物一并开源，供其他团队在此基础上继续做。帖中致谢的团队成员包括 Swarat Chaudhuri、Andrew Ferraiuolo、Anton Kovsharov、Tej Chajed、Sam Schlesinger、Karn Seth、Jonathan Katz 和 Charles Sutton，并感谢了 Signal 团队的合作。

Kohli 还补了一笔个人背景：二十多年前他以实习生身份入行，做的是复杂软件系统的一致性检查。这次的结果等于把他职业生涯起点上的那个难题，用现代 AI 做成了实际可用的工程。

读原文和官方博客时可以直接看两个点：开源仓库里的规范覆盖了哪些安全性质，以及博客对验证范围的限定。帖子证明的是关键安全性质，范围之外的部分不要替它扩大。

英文原文（完整保留）

Announcing a major milestone achieved by my team @GoogleDeepMind: we have proven key security properties of the machine code underlying the Signal messaging protocol. This is the first time the actual production code of a widely deployed protocol has been formally verified – by any method, with or without AI – and at this depth.

More than a research breakthrough, it’s a prime example of how frontier AI can be a force multiplier to counter the speed and scale of modern cyber threats.

Formal verification offers a higher standard of security; however, it’s extremely complex and time consuming, which is why in many cases it’s considered to be impractical. Similar efforts could take teams decades of combined effort. We did it in a month’s time.

Using AlphaProof Code, Google DeepMind's new agentic framework for formal verification that runs on LLMs including Gemini Carbon, we verified the Signal protocol down to the actual code running on devices. This milestone lays the groundwork for a future in which vital systems, from medical device firmware to power grid software, ship with mathematical proof that their protections work.

Reaching that future will take continued research from our community. In that spirit, we're open-sourcing our formal specifications and verification artifacts so others can build on this work.

On a personal note, this moment is of deep significance to me. I started my own research journey more than two decades ago as an intern working on checking the consistency of complex software systems. Seeing this grand challenge made practical with modern AI is extraordinary.

I'm extremely proud of my team members: Swarat Chaudhuri, Andrew Ferraiuolo, Anton Kovsharov, Tej Chajed, Sam Schlesinger, Karn Seth, Jonathan Katz, and Charles Sutton for taking on this grand challenge and delivering, and grateful to the Signal team for their partnership.
