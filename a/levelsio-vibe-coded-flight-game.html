levelsio 沙发上随手做的飞行游戏：从马里奥克隆到多人同飞，赚了 20 万美元

2025 年 2 月，levelsio 在沙发上用 Claude 折腾马里奥赛车式游戏没做成，转做飞行模拟器后赶上 Claude 3.7 Sonnet，多人同飞走红，一个月靠游戏内飞艇广告收入 8.5 万美元，总计 20 万美元，还顺手投了 Cursor、办了 Vibe Jam。

@levelsio（@levelsio）· 原帖 2026-11-17T16:12:50+00:00 · 收录 2026-11-19T08:35:00+08:00

2025 年 2 月前后，levelsio 坐在沙发上，腿上放着枕头，枕头上搁着笔记本电脑，像往常一样折腾一个点子：用 Claude 做一款马里奥赛车风格的游戏。折腾了几个小时，没做成。

Claude 3.5 时代，AI 还做不好游戏

当时是 Claude 3.5 Sonnet 的版本，AI 还不太会做游戏。没做成之后，他换了个方向，做飞行模拟器，起因是 Flight Simulator 2020 加载太慢，还总在更新，就像大多数现代游戏一样。这次进展好一些，他做出了一个方块风格的简易飞机游戏，边做边在 X 上发更新，不少人喜欢看。

质疑声里坚持做多人模式

做游戏的过程中，不断有人告诉他，用 AI 做不了游戏。等他想加多人模式，更多游戏开发者，连很有名的开发者也说，这绝无可能。

Claude 3.7 发布两天后，游戏突然能做了

转折出现在 Claude 3.7 Sonnet 发布之后。按他的说法，发布才两天，Claude 第一次真正能把游戏做得像样。他借此把多人模式做通了，最多时有几千人同时在线飞行，游戏迅速走红。

飞艇广告一个月赚 8.5 万美元，总计 20 万美元

走红之后，不少公司赞助了这款游戏，赞助形式是在游戏里放飞艇广告。游戏一个月收入 8.5 万美元，总计 20 万美元。数字来自作者本人的说法。

在 Cursor 里写代码，顺手投了 Cursor

这款游戏是在 Cursor 里写出来的。写代码期间，Cursor 的 Michael Truell（@mntruell）私信他，想听听改进 Cursor 的反馈，自我介绍时只说自己是“Cursor 开发人员”，语气很谦逊。他给了反馈，顺便问能不能投资 Cursor。对方说可以，他当周就把钱汇了过去。作者还引用了一句荷兰谚语，大意是不去尝试就永远没机会。

办 Vibe Jam，把注意力分给别人的游戏

看到这类 AI 做的游戏吸引了这么多关注，他想把注意力分给更多人的游戏，于是办了 Vibe Jam。这个比赛的名字取自 @karpathy 提出的 vibecoding 一词，参赛者用 AI 做游戏，吸引了 1000 多人参赛，参赛游戏的玩家超过 100 万人。

一年后 Cursor 被 SpaceX 收购，沙发项目带来几百万美元

按他的说法，一年之后，Cursor 被 SpaceX 收购了。他总结说，在沙发上漫无目的地折腾一款按任何标准都很烂的马里奥克隆游戏，最后带来了一连串正面连锁反应，按他自己的说法，这番折腾让他赚了几百万美元。他的结论是：让人放手折腾，看看最后能走到哪一步。

A good example of this effect is last year, around February 2025, I was sitting on my couch, with a pillow on my legs and my laptop on top of it (as per usual)

I was trying to make my own Mario Kart style game for hours with Claude, but of course it didn't work at all

This was the time of Claude 3.5 Sonnet and AI couldn't really make games well yet

Anyway I kept trying, and then tried to make a flight simulator, because I was so frustrated by Flight Simulator 2020 loading so slow with endless updates (like most modern games)

That ended up working a bit better and I built a basic blocky looking plane game and I'd tweet updates about it, and people seemed to like it

It was funny because as always with AI a lot of people on here kept telling me "no you can't just make a game with AI", then when I wanted to add multiplayer, even more game developers (even very famous ones) told me "you'll never ever make that work"

Then just 2 days in Claude 3.7 Sonnet came out and suddenly it was actually able to make games for the first time pretty well

And thanks to that, I did get it to work, and we had thousands of people flying at the same time in multiplayer (see pic) and it went really viral

Lots of companies ended up sponsoring the game itself with in-game blimps (remember?) and it made $85,000 in just a month (and $200K total)

I coded that game in Cursor and then @mntruell messaged me my DMs to ask feedback how to improve Cursor (introducing himself as just a "Cursor dev" so humble), so I gave feedback

In that convo I asked if I could ever invest in Cursor (as Dutch people say "not shot is always a miss") and he said sure, and then I wired the money the same week

Meanwhile seeing how much attention these vibe coded games made I thought it'd be nice to direct that to other people's games, so I made Vibe Jam (named after @karpathy's vibecoding term): a game dev competition for vibe coded games, which got 1000+ people to make games and over 1 million people playing the games

Then a year later, Cursor ended up being acquired by SpaceX!

So me messing around on the couch with no goal whatsoever on some stupid game had lots of positive second order effects and literally made me millions, but it'd be hard to imagine that if you'd see me sit there in February 2025 on the couch working on a Mario Kart clone that by any measure sucked

So I guess the point is, let people mess around and see where things end up!!!
